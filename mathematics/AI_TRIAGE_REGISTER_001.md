## AI slop / unsupported narrative quarantine

The following language must not be promoted as a factual result without the listed evidence:

- “universal harmonic pattern recognition engine” — metaphor/hypothesis, not a demonstrated universal theorem.
- “solved group theory through gradient descent” — unsupported interpretation until mechanistic evidence and controlled replication are supplied.
- “three miracles of CP8” — rhetorical framing; replace in technical summaries with neutral phenomenon names.
- “phase transition at epochs 8–15” — requires training curves, multiple seeds, change-point methodology, and controls.
- “attention heads specialize in D6 / golden-ratio scaling” — requires head-level probes, interventions, controls, and independent replication.
- “autonomous mathematical discovery” — must be operationally defined and compared against memorization, leakage, and baseline models.
- “every step is mathematical; every threshold has a derivation” — contradicted by the arithmetic and gate-order errors in the source PDF.
- “semantic canonicalization makes different wording hash identically” — undefined and technically false for ordinary cryptographic hashing.
- “physical resonance frequencies determine epistemic safety thresholds” — no causal model or validated measurement bridge supplied.
- “crop-circle patterns contain messages” — no objective encoding or blind decoding evidence supplied.
- “human intent / love-bias creates coherence” — symbolic interpretation, not an established physical mechanism.

This quarantine does not erase the source text. It prevents unverified prose from being mistaken for validated science.

Rules:
- Do not infer missing equations.
- Do not repair source errors silently.
- Cite exact repository paths and line/page ranges.
- Treat absence of a source artifact as missing evidence, not proof that it never existed.
- Recompute arithmetic independently.
- Use at least one negative control for any pattern-detection claim.
- Separate mathematical validity from whether the formula is original.
- Separate code execution from scientific validity.
- Do not count repeated AI agreement as independent confirmation if the systems share the same prompt, source text, or model lineage.
- Identify prompt contamination and common-source dependence.
- Preserve minority and dissenting reviews.
- Do not promote an item to ESTABLISHED without reproducible evidence.
